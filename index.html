The season

Fifteen fixtures, two nights

You play everyone in your division three times. Every league night is a doubleheader: two fixtures back to back against two different clubs, about two and a half hours of pickleball.

League nights run at two indoor home grounds, and every club plays each ground equally. Venues announced before opening night.
